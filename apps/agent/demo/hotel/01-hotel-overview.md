# Holiday Hotel, Houston

Holiday Hotel is a friendly 120-room hotel in Houston, Texas. It opened in 2019 and the lobby was renovated in 2024.

## The front desk

The front desk is open 24 hours a day, every day. The team speaks English and Spanish. Bell service and luggage storage are free for guests, before check-in and after check-out.

## Every room has

Free wifi, air conditioning, blackout curtains, a coffee maker, an in-room safe, a hair dryer, an iron and ironing board, and a flat-screen TV. All rooms are smoke-free.

## Accessibility

There are 8 accessible rooms, with roll-in showers, grab bars, lowered switches and visual door alarms. All floors have elevators. There is accessible parking by the front door. Ask for an accessible room when booking and the team will hold one.

## Holiday Rewards

Holiday Rewards is free to join at check-in. Members earn 10 points for every dollar spent at the hotel, and 5,000 points is a free night. Members get a late check-out until 1 pm.

## Where we are

The hotel is in Houston, Texas. For restaurants, attractions, golf and directions in the area, the agent looks them up on the web.
