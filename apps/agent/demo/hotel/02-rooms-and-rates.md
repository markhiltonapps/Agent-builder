# Rooms and rates

Rates are per night before tax. The booking system gives the exact price for any dates, so always check it for the real figure. A 12 percent room tax is added to every stay. There is no resort fee.

## The rooms

- Standard King: one king bed, sleeps 2, quiet courtyard view, desk. From $149 a night.
- Standard Double Queen: two queen beds, sleeps 4, desk and mini fridge. From $169 a night.
- Deluxe King with Balcony: one king bed, sleeps 2, private balcony with a city view, sitting area and a soaking tub. From $199 a night.
- Family Suite: a king bed and a sofa bed plus a second bedroom with two twin beds, sleeps 6, kitchenette and two bathrooms. From $279 a night.
- Penthouse Suite: top floor, one king bed, sleeps up to 3, wraparound terrace, panoramic city views, jetted tub and a separate living room. From $449 a night. There is only one, so it is often sold out.

## When the rate changes

- Friday and Saturday nights cost $30 more per room ($60 more for the Penthouse Suite).
- Thanksgiving week, November 25 to 28: $40 more per night.
- Christmas, December 23 to 26: $50 more per night.
- New Year's Eve, December 31: $60 more, and a two-night minimum stay applies.
- November 20 to 22, a large convention is in town and the hotel is sold out.

## Promo codes

- WELCOME10: 10 percent off the room rate for first-time guests.
- MILITARY: 15 percent off for military and veterans. Show ID at check-in.
- AAA: 10 percent off for auto club members. Show the card at check-in.

## Paying

A deposit of the first night plus tax is charged when the room is booked, to guarantee it. The rest is paid at check-in. We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Deposits are only ever taken for a booking, and never by email or text.

## Children and extra beds

Children under 12 stay free in their parents' room. A crib is free. A rollaway bed is $20 per night (not available in the Standard King).
