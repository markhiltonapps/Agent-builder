# Hotel policies

## Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 3 pm. Check-out is by 11 am. Guests must be 21 or older to check in, and need a photo ID and a credit card. A hold of $50 per night is placed on the card for incidentals and released at check-out.

## Early check-in and late check-out

Early check-in from 12 noon is free; the front desk holds luggage if the room is not ready. Late check-out until 1 pm is free. Until 3 pm costs $30, if the room is not needed. Holiday Rewards members get 1 pm late check-out automatically.

## Cancellations and changes

A booking can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before 3 pm on the check-in day. After that the deposit, which is the first night, is kept. A refund goes back to the original card in 3 to 5 business days. Dates and rooms can be changed when the hotel has space, and the new rate applies. A no-show forfeits the deposit.

## Pets

Dogs under 40 pounds are welcome, one per room, for a one-time $50 fee per stay. Service animals stay free and are always welcome.

## Smoking

The hotel is completely smoke-free, including balconies. A $250 cleaning fee applies if smoking is found in a room. Smoking is allowed in the designated area by the pool.

## Parking

Self-parking is $15 per night. Valet parking is $28 per night. Electric car charging stations in the garage are free for guests. Oversize vehicles can park in the lot across the street.

## Quiet hours

Quiet hours are 10 pm to 7 am. Rooms on the fifth and sixth floors are the quietest and can be requested.

## Groups and events

Bookings of more than 8 guests, 9 or more rooms, weddings, meetings and other events are handled by the sales team, who call back within one business day.

## Lost and found

Left something behind? The team will look and ship items for a small shipping fee.
