# Dining and amenities

## Breakfast

A free hot breakfast is served for hotel guests every morning from 6:30 am to 10:30 am at the Harbor Grill, with eggs, waffles, fresh fruit, pastries and a coffee bar. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options are available.

## Harbor Grill

The hotel restaurant serves dinner from 5 pm to 9:30 pm. It is known for grilled fish, steaks and a seasonal menu, with a children's menu. Hotel guests get a free dessert. Reservations are recommended on weekends and the team can book a table.

## Lighthouse Lounge

The lobby bar is open from 4 pm to midnight, with local beers, cocktails and small plates. Happy hour is 4 pm to 6 pm daily.

## Room service and coffee

Room service runs from 7 am to 10 pm. The lobby coffee kiosk is open from 6 am to 11 am.

## Pool and hot tub

The heated outdoor pool and hot tub are open from 7 am to 10 pm. Towels are provided. There is a shallow area for children and an adults-only hour from 8 pm.

## Fitness room

The fitness room is open 24 hours with cardio machines, free weights and yoga mats.

## Sea Salt Spa

The spa is open from 9 am to 7 pm, by appointment. A 60-minute massage is $120, a facial is $95, and couples massages are available.

## Other services

- Free cruiser bike rentals for guests, from the front desk.
- Free beach chairs, umbrellas and towels at the front desk.
- Free self-service laundry on the second floor.
- Business center in the lobby, with printing.
- A gift shop in the lobby with snacks, sunscreen and souvenirs.
- A lobby ATM.
- Airport shuttle: $25 per person each way, every 30 minutes from 5 am to 11 pm. It can be added to any reservation.
