# Around Port Marlow

Distances are from the hotel. Walking time is about 20 minutes a mile.

## Getting here and around

- Marlow Regional Airport (MRL): 14 miles, about 25 minutes by car. The hotel shuttle is $25 per person, every 30 minutes from 5 am to 11 pm. A taxi or rideshare is about $35.
- The Marlow Trolley loops Old Town, the beach and the pier every 20 minutes from 9 am to 9 pm. A ride is $1.50. The stop is outside the front door.
- Free bike rentals from the front desk. The whole town is flat and easy to bike.

## Beaches

- Marlow Beach: 0.2 miles, a four-minute walk. Lifeguards from Memorial Day to Labor Day. The hotel lends chairs, umbrellas and towels.
- Dune Cove: 3 miles, quieter, with tide pools. Best for a walk at sunrise.
- Seabird Island: a 10-minute ferry from Harbor Marina, every hour from 10 am to 5 pm. Wildlife and walking trails.

## Things to do

- Pier and boardwalk, 0.4 miles: arcade, mini golf ($9), a small Ferris wheel ($6), and fresh doughnuts.
- Lighthouse Point, 1.1 miles: tours $12, Wednesday to Sunday, 10 am to 4 pm. 120 steps to a great view.
- Marlow Aquarium, 1.5 miles: $22 adults, $14 children, 9 am to 5 pm. A favorite with kids, with a touch tank.
- Old Town historic district, 0.7 miles: boutiques, art galleries and the 1790s Customs House.
- Marlow Maritime Museum, 0.8 miles: $10, 10 am to 5 pm, closed Mondays.
- Saturday Farmers Market in the Old Town square, 8 am to 1 pm.
- Harbor Marina, 0.6 miles: whale-watching tours from April to October ($48 adults, two hours) and sunset sails ($55) all year.
- Marlow Nature Preserve, 4 miles: trails and kayak rentals ($35 for two hours).
- Dunes Golf Links, 6 miles: 18 holes, from $65. Clubs can be rented.
- Splash park for small children, 0.5 miles, free, open May to September.

## Where to eat

- Brew & Bean Café, 0.2 miles: coffee and breakfast, 6:30 am to 3 pm.
- The Salt Cellar, 0.3 miles: seafood, a special-occasion dinner, 5 pm to 10 pm, reservations recommended. About $$$.
- Marlow Creamery, 0.3 miles: ice cream, noon to 10 pm.
- Sunrise Taqueria, 0.4 miles: casual Mexican, 10 am to 9 pm. $.
- Luna Trattoria, 0.5 miles: Italian pasta and wood-fired pizza, 11 am to 10 pm. $$.
- The Anchor Pub, 0.5 miles: pub food, with live music on Friday and Saturday nights.
- Dockside Fish Shack, 0.6 miles: casual fried fish and chowder, 11 am to 8 pm. $.
- Green Tide Kitchen, 0.6 miles: vegetarian and vegan, 11 am to 9 pm. $$.
- Koyo Sushi, 0.7 miles: sushi, 5 pm to 10 pm, closed Mondays. $$.

## Useful places

- Marlow Market grocery: 0.4 miles, 7 am to 11 pm.
- Harbor Pharmacy: 0.5 miles, 8 am to 9 pm.
- Marlow General Hospital with a 24-hour emergency room: 2.4 miles, about 7 minutes by car. An urgent care clinic is 2.1 miles away.
- A laundromat and a dry cleaner in Old Town. The hotel also has free self-service laundry.

## Events coming up

- Port Marlow Marathon, November 21. The hotel is sold out November 20 to 22 and roads near the beach close in the morning.
- Holiday Lights Parade, December 5, 6 pm, Old Town.
- Harbor Boat Parade, December 19, 7 pm, best seen from the pier.
- New Year's Eve fireworks over the harbor, December 31 at midnight, a short walk away.

## Good to know

Winters are mild, about 45 to 60 degrees. Summers are in the 80s and the beach is best from June to September, when the water is warmest. Bring layers in the evening.
