# Location and the area

Holiday Hotel is in Houston, Texas. The hotel's own details (rooms, rates, policies, dining) are in the other documents.

## Anything about the area

For anything about the surrounding area, such as restaurants, golf courses, attractions, shopping, nightlife, events, directions, traffic, the weather or the airports, the agent looks it up on the web while it talks and says where the answer came from. It does not guess.

## Airports

Houston has two main airports, George Bush Intercontinental (IAH) and William P. Hobby (HOU). The hotel shuttle serves both by reservation, at $25 per person each way. The front desk confirms the pickup time. Ask for the flight number and arrival time when adding the shuttle.

## Getting around

Self-parking is $15 a night and valet is $28 a night. The front desk can call a taxi or arrange a rideshare pickup at the main entrance.
